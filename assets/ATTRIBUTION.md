# Asset Attribution

## Mogao Cave 328 · Kneeling Attendant Bodhisattva

- **Original object:** Kneeling Attendant Bodhisattva, late 7th century, Tang dynasty
- **Origin:** Mogao Cave 328, Dunhuang, Gansu, China
- **Collection object:** Harvard Art Museums / Arthur M. Sackler Museum, `1924.70`
- **Model author:** Harvard Art Museums
- **Model source:** [Sketchfab model 129635ad396947a7975c9e5ba6095f32](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kneeling-attendant-bodhisattva-129635ad396947a7975c9e5ba6095f32)
- **Collection record:** [Harvard object record](https://hvrd.art/o/303672)
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution (CC Attribution / CC BY)

The local photogrammetric asset is stored at:

`assets/models/mogao-c328-source/Kneeling_Attendant_Bodhisattva-129635ad/`

The scene uses the downloaded glTF geometry and its original 8K JPEG detail texture. The scan's baked vertex colors remain the primary albedo so the painted surface is not replaced by a high-frequency procedural treatment; the JPEG is assigned as a very low-amplitude micro-surface bump source. The model is uniformly fitted into the scene and rotated for the camera; no sculptural geometry is procedurally recreated.

Attribution should remain with any published copy, still, or render of this project.
